Cookie Policy

Last Updated: July 17, 2026

Velocity ("we", "our", or "us") uses standard cookies and tracking web technologies to improve your experience on our real estate lead routing platform, deliver secure sessions, and analyze anonymous site traffic.

1. What are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your computer or mobile device when you load our pages. They enable the website to recognize your session state and remember vital application preferences (such as your routing dashboard layout or login status).

2. Types of Cookies We Use

We utilize strictly necessary session cookies to maintain user authentication and system integrity. We also employ anonymous analytical tracking cookies to trace demographic page flow and optimize loading speeds.

3. Managing Your Preferences

You can configure your browser settings to reject or delete cookies entirely. Please note that restricting essential cookies may impact specific performance aspects of your routing environment.
