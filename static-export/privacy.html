Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 17, 2026

Velocity ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your business and personal data. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, store, share, and protect your information when you interact with our real estate lead routing platform, our website, and any related services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect direct information that you provide to us, such as your name, business email, phone number, brokerage details, CRM software preference, and monthly lead volumes when you schedule a demo or sign up for a pilot program. We also process transient end-user lead details (such as names, inquiry types, and contact info) that are routed through our platform on behalf of your brokerage.

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected information exclusively to provision your custom environment, optimize lightning lead routing paths, secure system integrations, provide analytical metrics, and deliver customer support. We do not sell or lease your brokerage data or lead information to third parties.

3. Data Security and Standards

We implement industry-standard administrative, physical, and technological security controls, including complete end-to-end encryption (SSL/TLS) for data in transit and at rest, to safeguard your data from unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure.
